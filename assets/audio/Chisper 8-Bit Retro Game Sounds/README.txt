8-Bit Retro Game Sounds: Coins, Jumps, Power-ups and Hits
by Qvantor, made in Chisper (https://chisper.qvantor.org)

24 retro 8-bit sound effects for platformers, arcade and pixel art games.
CC0: free for any use, commercial too, no credit needed (see LICENSE.txt).

Every sound was made in Chisper, a free wavetable synth that runs in the browser, from the sounds of an old game
console: pulse waves of 50, 25 and 12.5%, a triangle and shift-register (LFSR) noise. No samples.
Every sound below has its preset: open the link, press a key and the synth plays the sound, jingles included.
Change the pitch, the length or the echo and record your own version.

Formats
  WAV/  48 kHz, 24-bit, stereo
  OGG/  Ogg Vorbis (q6), the same sounds
Each file starts right at the sound. Loudness matched: the loudest 100 ms at about -15 LUFS, peaks under -1 dBTP.
